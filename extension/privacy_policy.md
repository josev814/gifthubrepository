Gift Registry Extension — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025-11-06

This extension helps you add items to your gift registry. We take your privacy seriously.

What we collect:
- No browsing history is stored by the extension.
- The extension reads the active tab's URL and title only at the moment you click “Add”.
- If you choose to log in via the extension, we store an authentication token in browser storage so the extension can call the API.

How we use data:
- URLs and titles you add are sent to your personal registry on our backend (api.example.com).
- We may send you email notifications related to reservations (SendGrid).

Third parties:
- We use SendGrid to send transactional email. Please review SendGrid's privacy policy.

Security:
- Tokens are stored in local extension storage and are not transmitted to third parties.
- You can revoke access by revoking tokens on your account page.

Contact:
- For questions or to request deletion of data, email privacy@example.com
